About the Company

Operation MarketMap is a lead generation and market research firm helping businesses tap into new opportunities. Our focus lies in data-driven insights and precision outreach strategies. We believe in building strong databases for better business decisions.

About Project K

Project K is designed to gather verified company data from different industries to build a powerful database for business outreach. This internship offers an opportunity to contribute meaningfully while learning real-world market research and lead generation techniques.

Target Industries

  • Manufacturing
  • Wholesale & Distribution
  • Retail & E-commerce
  • FMCG
  • Healthcare (Clinics, Labs)
  • Pharmaceuticals
  • Education
  • Automotive

Internship Guidelines

Interns must use the AppSheet app to input company details. Please ensure:

  • Accurate and complete company information
  • LinkedIn pages or decision-maker names (like Director, Sales Head) if available
  • No duplicate entries (the app restricts this)
  • Minimum 100 entries/day for 10 days (1000 total)
  • Bonus incentives for exceeding 100 entries/day

Incentives

Each intern will be eligible for performance-based incentives. Completing the base 1000 entries over 10 days guarantees a certificate and base incentive. Any entries beyond the 100 per day target will be rewarded with bonus incentives.
