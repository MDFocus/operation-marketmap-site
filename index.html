About the Project

ProjectK is an initiative under the Operation MarketMap internship program focused on collecting and analyzing market data from industries such as FMCG, Manufacturing, Retail, Education, and Healthcare. Interns will be responsible for gathering, validating, and submitting data using our AppSheet-based platform.
