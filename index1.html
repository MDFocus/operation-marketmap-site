🌐 Operation MarketMap – Internship Project: ProjectK

🧩 About the Program

Operation MarketMap is a research-driven initiative designed to map and analyze key industrial markets across India. Under this flagship program, ProjectK focuses on collecting structured and verified market intelligence data through an internship model.

This initiative spans across five core sectors:

  • FMCG (Fast Moving Consumer Goods)
  • Manufacturing
  • Retail
  • Education
  • Healthcare

The project leverages an AppSheet-powered digital platform to ensure real-time syncing, smart dashboards, duplicate-checking, and seamless validation of all data entries.

🎯 Objectives of ProjectK

  • Collect real-world data on companies from multiple sectors
  • Validate company details including contact info, location, type, and services
  • Organize regional industrial intelligence for strategic use
  • Empower interns with a working understanding of data research, analytics, and lead generation
  • Build an accessible and expandable database of businesses

🧪 How It Works

Interns will:

  • Receive an industry/region allocation
  • Collect data (Company Name, Contact Person, Email, Phone, Website, Address, Industry)
  • Submit leads via the AppSheet mobile/web platform
  • Get real-time updates on progress and rankings
  • Be evaluated on performance, accuracy, and consistency

  • 🧠 AI-powered Duplicate Entry Prevention
